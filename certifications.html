Our competitive edge

The only Saudi NACE-qualified provider.

IWS is the only regional provider whose casing and casing-head welding methodology was developed and qualified together with a major operator's technical organization specifically to meet sour-service requirements — and it passed every qualifying test.

For operators, that qualification is the difference between a weld that merely looks right and one that is documented, temperature-controlled and traceable — able to hold on a live, sour-service well without cracking.
