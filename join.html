Careers

Build wells that hold.

IWS is the only Saudi NACE-qualified provider of casing and casing-head welding. We're looking for welders, technicians and engineers who take pride in work that has to be right the first time, on a live well.

Why work here

Specialist work, done to a standard.

We operate where the margin for error is zero — sour-service wells, live platforms, code-traceable welds. That demands people who are trained, certified and trusted, and we invest accordingly.

◆

Real responsibility

Small, expert crews. Your work is signed off by NDE and goes straight into well integrity — no busywork, no padding.

▲

Certification & growth

We support NACE, ASME and API qualification and keep your tickets current. Skills here travel anywhere in the industry.

●

Safety first, always

Zero lost-time incidents across 1,000+ jobs. That record is built by people who are empowered to stop the job.

Open roles

Where we're hiring.

Roles are based in the Eastern Province, Saudi Arabia, with rotation to onshore and offshore sites. If your role isn't listed, we still want to hear from qualified specialists.

NACE-Qualified Welder

Casing and casing-head welding to qualified WPS, with controlled preheat and PWHT. Sour-service experience preferred.

Field · Rotation Apply

Wellhead Technician

Cold cutting, hot tapping, wellhead removal and re-installation. Mechanical aptitude and pressure-control awareness essential.

Field · Rotation Apply

NDE / QA Inspector

Visual, MT and UT inspection of weldments; building and maintaining the documentation pack for each job. Certification required.

Field · Office Apply

Welding / Wellhead Engineer

Prepare procedures and weld maps, qualify WPS/PQR, and provide technical supervision on site. Degree plus field experience.

Office · Field Apply

HSE Officer

Drive our zero-LTI culture on site — risk assessment, permit-to-work, toolbox talks and incident-free delivery.
